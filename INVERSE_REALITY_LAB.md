# Inverse Reality Lab — Computational Egyptology & Inverse Causal Construction

Public research laboratory: **https://deepanalytica.cl/labs/inverse-reality-lab/**

## Research focus

Inverse Reality Lab studies whether a finished monument can be treated as a terminal physical state from which families of compatible predecessor states are reconstructed under geometry, material, lithology, gravity, topology, structural stability, accessibility, archaeology and evidence constraints.

Primary test case: **Khufu's Great Pyramid at Giza**.

## Scientific status

Exploratory research program. It does not claim that a unique pyramid construction sequence has been recovered. General-relativistic Vaidya/Kerr models are used only as mathematical laboratories for accessibility topology, not as mechanisms of Egyptian construction.
