# Inverse Reality Lab

**Chrono-causal reconstruction of Khufu's Great Pyramid from a terminal physical state.**

This public research laboratory explores **Inverse Causal Construction (ICC)** and an **Inverse Reality Ontology (IRO)**: a framework for inferring families of physically and archaeologically compatible construction histories from a finished object's geometry, materials, lithology, internal voids, load paths, provenance and evidence.

## Scientific caution

The framework is exploratory. It does **not** claim that a unique construction sequence has been recovered, nor that general-relativistic black-hole physics explains pyramid construction. Relativistic models are used only as cross-domain mathematical laboratories for accessibility topology and inverse inference.
